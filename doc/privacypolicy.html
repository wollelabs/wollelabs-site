Privacy Policy — Last updated: April 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SnoopTheDay handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. The short version: we collect no personal data and everything stays on your device.

Who We Are

SnoopTheDay is developed by Wolfgang Strasser, a private individual.

Contact: wollelabs@gmail.com

Data We Collect

No data collected

SnoopTheDay does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. There are no user accounts, no login, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

All data used by the app (contacts you add, calendar entries) remains exclusively on your device.

Permissions

👤
Contacts

SnoopTheDay may request access to your device contacts to let you select people for milestone tracking. Contact data is only read locally and is never uploaded or shared.

📅
Calendar

SnoopTheDay may request access to your device calendar to create or delete reminder entries for upcoming milestones. Calendar data is never read beyond what is needed to manage these entries, and nothing is transmitted externally.

Third-Party Services

SnoopTheDay is distributed via the Google Play Store. Google may collect certain technical and usage data as part of their platform services (e.g. crash reports, install statistics). This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

No other third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks are integrated in the current version.

Advertising

SnoopTheDay currently contains no advertisements. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly and you will be informed through the app store listing.

Children's Privacy

SnoopTheDay is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Since no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions about your rights under GDPR or other applicable privacy laws, please contact us at wollelabs@gmail.com.
